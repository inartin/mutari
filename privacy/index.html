Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to Mutari (com.inartin.mutari).

Data collection

Mutari does not collect or transmit personal information or device data. The game has no accounts, advertising, analytics, online services, or third-party data-collection SDKs.

Local data

Mutari stores only your highest survival score and audio preference locally on your device. This information never leaves your device and is not accessible to the developer.

Permissions

Mutari does not request access to the internet, location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, media, notifications, or device identifiers.

Sharing and selling

Mutari does not share, sell, or disclose user data to third parties.

Retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall Mutari. Because this information is not transmitted, the developer cannot access or delete it remotely.

Children's privacy

Mutari does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Policy changes

This policy may be updated if Mutari's data practices change. The latest version and effective date will always appear on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, email privacy@example.com.
